## Abstract

**HealthScope** is a full-lifecycle public health analytics and risk prediction platform designed to transform healthcare datasets into meaningful insights through data science, machine learning, and interactive visualization.

The project uses publicly available datasets related to diseases such as **diabetes and heart disease** to analyze important health factors including age, blood pressure, glucose levels, BMI, cholesterol, and lifestyle indicators.

The system follows a complete data science workflow involving **data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, feature engineering, machine learning model development, and interactive visualization**. Multiple machine learning algorithms, including **Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest**, will be trained and evaluated using appropriate performance metrics.

An interactive dashboard will present **health trends, risk-factor analysis, model performance, and individual risk predictions** in an accessible and user-friendly format. HealthScope aims to demonstrate how data-driven techniques can support **early risk identification, public health pattern analysis, and informed decision-making** through a practical healthcare analytics platform.

## Expected Outcome

HealthScope is expected to provide a practical and user-friendly platform for analyzing public health data and identifying potential health risks. The system will combine **data analytics, machine learning, and visualization** to make complex healthcare datasets easier to understand.

The project will demonstrate how a complete data science pipeline can be applied to healthcare analytics, from **raw data preparation to risk prediction and interactive visualization**.
